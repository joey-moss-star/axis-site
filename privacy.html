Privacy policy

Axis · last updated 12 October 2026

Who this covers

Axis is personal software used only by its owner, Joey Moss, to run his DJ business. It has no other users and is not offered to the public. The only account data it handles is the owner's own.

What Axis accesses

With the owner's permission, given on Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page, Axis reads his email (Gmail or Outlook) and his calendar events. It uses them to find bookings, customer replies and receipts, to show his calendar alongside his gigs, and to prepare drafts and plans that he reviews. It does not mark, move or delete email. Calendar changes and outgoing email only ever happen after the owner approves each one.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are encrypted there and are never shown, logged or included in backups. Axis runs no shared server and has no database outside the owner's computer.

Sharing

Axis does not sell, rent or share any data, does not use it for advertising, and does not transfer it to anyone. No person other than the owner reads it.

Google user data

Axis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account in Axis at any time, which deletes the stored sign-in, and can withdraw access from his Google account or Microsoft account. Imported data can be deleted from his computer.

Contact

Joey Moss, through the support email shown on the Google or Microsoft sign-in screen for Axis.
