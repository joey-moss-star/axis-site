Axis

A private business assistant for one self-employed DJ.

Axis is personal software run by its owner, Joey Moss, on his own computer. It helps him run his DJ business: it reads his business and personal email and calendars to find bookings, replies and receipts, and prepares drafts and plans for him to approve. It is not a public service and has no other users.

When Joey connects his Google or Microsoft account, Axis reads his mail and calendar only for this purpose, keeps the data on his own computer, and never sells or shares it.
